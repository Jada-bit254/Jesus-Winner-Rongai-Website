FROM OUR SHEPHERD

Welcome Home.
There Is a Place for You Here.

Welcome to Jesus Winner Ministry Rongai. It is my joy to welcome you to a church family where we believe that every person matters to God and every life carries a divine purpose.

Our desire is to see people encounter Jesus Christ, grow deeper in their relationship with God, discover their purpose, and become everything God has called them to be.

Through the preaching of God's Word, prayer, worship, discipleship, evangelism, healing, deliverance, and practical service to our community, we desire to be a church where faith comes alive and lives are transformed.

Whether you are visiting us for the first time, returning after many years, or searching for a church family where you can grow, we want you to know that you are welcome here.

Come with expectation. Come with faith. Come ready to encounter God.

Pastor Isaiah Lead Pastor, Jesus Winner Ministry Rongai
