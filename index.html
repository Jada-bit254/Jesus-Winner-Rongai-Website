Our Ministry

    Children Ministry

    We are committed to nurturing the next generation in the faith and providing a safe, loving environment for children to grow and learn about God.

  • Youth Ministry

    • Our youth ministry is designed to engage young people in meaningful ways, helping them to develop a strong foundation in their faith and encouraging them to live out their beliefs in their daily lives.

  • Praise and Worship Team

    • Our praise and worship team leads the congregation in heartfelt worship, creating an atmosphere where people can encounter God and experience His presence.

  • Men's Fellowship

    • Our men's fellowship provides a space for men to grow in their faith, build meaningful relationships, and support one another in their spiritual journeys.

  • Women's Fellowship

    • Our women's fellowship is a vibrant community where women can come together to grow in their faith, support one another, and make a positive impact in their families and communities.

  • Community Outreach

    • We are committed to serving our community through various outreach programs, including feeding the hungry, supporting the needy, and engaging in acts of kindness and service.

  • Prayer Ministry

    • Our prayer ministry is dedicated to interceding for the needs of our congregation, community, and the world, believing in the power of prayer to bring about change and transformation.

  • Media Ministry

    • Our media ministry is responsible for capturing and sharing the message of Jesus Winner Ministry Rongai through various media platforms, including social media, video production, and live streaming of our services.

  • Evangelism Ministry

    • Our evangelism ministry is dedicated to spreading the gospel and making disciples, reaching out to those who have not yet heard the message of salvation.
