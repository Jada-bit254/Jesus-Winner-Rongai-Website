Who we are

A place to encounter God, belong, and grow.

Jesus Winner Ministry Rongai is a vibrant Christian family committed to living out the love of Christ through worship, discipleship, and mission.

Our Mission

To see lives transformed by the Gospel, rooted in faith, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and filled with purpose to serve God and humanity.

Our Vision

To build a Christ-centered community where everyone finds belonging, spiritual growth, and a clear path to fulfilling their divine purpose.

Faith

We teach the truth of God’s Word and cultivate a life of trust in Him.

Hope

We point people to the promises of God and encourage perseverance through every season.

Service

We seek to love our community through outreach, kindness, and practical support.
